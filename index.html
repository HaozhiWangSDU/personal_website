关于我 About Me

您好！我是王浩智 (Haozhi Wang)，目前就读于山东大学基础医学院临床医学专业。我热衷于医学与数据科学的交叉前沿研究，当前的主要学术方向致力于计算神经发育电生理学 (Computational Developmental Electrophysiology) 以及基于脑电图与事件相关电位 (EEG/ERP) 的ADHD与ASD计算表型研究。我希望结合临床神经发育表型理解、规范的EEG/MNE分析流程与可解释性机器学习算法，开发用于神经发育障碍辅助评估、疗效预测与数字生物标志物提取的临床转化工具。

Hello! I am Haozhi Wang, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Clinical Medicine at the School of Basic Medical Sciences, Shandong University. Driven by a strong passion for bridging clinical medicine with computational intelligence, my primary research focus is centered on Computational Developmental Electrophysiology and EEG/ERP-based computational phenotyping of neurodevelopmental disorders (such as ADHD and ASD). I aim to integrate clinical neurodevelopmental phenotyping, rigorous EEG/MNE analysis workflows, and explainable machine learning/AI models to develop translational clinical tools for subtyping classification, treatment response prediction, and digital biomarker discovery.
